He actively supports

Fondation Brigitte Bardot

The Brigitte Bardot Foundation currently takes care of more than 7,000 animals, including nearly 3,800 farm animals and 1,000 equines, in its 3 shelters and partner structures that make up the Arche de BB. Brigitte Bardot has dedicated her life for the animals in need. “Cette grande dame” deserves our highest respect.

Animaux en Péril

The main mission of the association is to welcome and take care of farm animals and horses that have been mistreated or seriously neglected, as well as wandering animals.

Let's prepare spring for the insects !

Protecting the insects is protecting our ecosystem

The Smile Train

Ronny Flas actively and regularly supports “The Smile Train” association whose vision is to give every child with a cleft the opportunity for a healthy, productive life.
More info on: https://www.smiletrain.org/our-cause

Le Parc Australien

Ronny Flas supports “Le Parc Australien” for the financing of the transport to Australia of the pockets for baby kangaroos who survived the dramatic Australian fires. If you can, please do it too !!!

SRPA Eupen

Ronny Flas officially supports the Animal Protection Agency of Eupen (SRPA Eupen). If you can, please do it too !!!

King Baudouin Foundation

Ronny Flas officially supports the King Baudouin Foundation in order to finance respirators that are needed by hospitals to take care of people who have contracted the COVID-19 virus. If you can, please do it too !!!
